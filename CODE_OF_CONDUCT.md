# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient with people who are new to Odoo or to AI tooling.
- Accepting constructive criticism, and giving it about code rather than about people.
- Focusing on what is best for the people whose ERP systems run this software.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  explicit permission.
- Posting someone else's database contents, keys or customer data in an issue.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **devops@itauco.mn**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this code, and
may ban contributors temporarily or permanently for behaviour they consider inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
