ASLA Client Agent

An AI agent inside your own Odoo — free and open source (LGPL-3)

Your team asks Odoo questions in Discuss and gets answers grounded in your own configuration. Support tickets are raised and answered inside your Odoo. Work that changes your data is planned, dry-run and approved by a person before it runs.

Offline

The model runs next to your Odoo. Nothing leaves your network. Grounded answers need an asla_odoo_ai service; without it the agent falls back to a plain local model and says so in its reply.

Online

Uses ASLA's larger model and Odoo knowledge through the hub. It sends your question and the structure of the models involved — field names and types — never the rows.

What it may touch in your database

  • Reading is automatic.
  • Anything that changes data is planned, dry-run and shown to a person, who approves or rejects it.
  • Users, permissions and accounting entries are never changed automatically.
  • That list lives in your Odoo, not ours — and the module is open source, so you can read exactly what it does.

Requirements

Odoo 18, on your own server or Odoo.sh. Odoo Online cannot install custom modules.
